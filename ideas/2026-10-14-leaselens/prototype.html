Likely unenforceable mid-lease

The addendum removes a material term you already paid for (reserved parking, included in rent) and tries to override your lease's own modification clause. Under Colorado law, landlords generally cannot impose unilateral mid-lease changes to material terms. They can propose it for your next renewal — you're not obligated to sign now.

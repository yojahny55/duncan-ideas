Tenant Rights

Know your leverage by city

Rent control, notice periods, and caps vary wildly. Here's what renters often don't know.

🇨🇺 Florida

Tampa / Orlando / Miami

No state rent control. No cap on increases. Landlord can raise rent any amount at renewal.

Leverage: 60-day notice required for non-renewal. Use market data as your argument — it's all you have.
🗽 New York

NYC Rent-Stabilized

~1M apartments are rent-stabilized. Increases set annually by RGB (2026: 2.25% 1yr, 4.0% 2yr).

Leverage: If stabilized, landlord CANNOT charge above RGB increase. Check your registration status — many don't know they're covered.
🌲 West Coast

Seattle / Portland / SF

Seattle: 60-day notice for any increase. SF: rent control covers most pre-1979 buildings. Portland: 90-day notice, 10% cap.

Leverage: 3 of the 4 biggest West Coast cities have increase caps or rent boards. File a petition if yours exceeds the cap.

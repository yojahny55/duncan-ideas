# Ember 🔥 — Perimenopause & Menopause Companion

> **"You're not crazy. You're not alone. And there's a pattern hiding in your data."**

## The Problem

**50M+ US women** are in perimenopause or menopause right now. **1.3B globally by 2030.** And the medical system is failing them:

- **1 in 5 women** experience symptoms for **more than a year** before a healthcare provider confirms they're in perimenopause (Clue, 2026)
- **Most doctors receive <2 hours of menopause training** in medical school (The Menopause Society, 2025)
- **73% of women** manage symptoms without adequate support (Women's Health Initiative, 2025)
- Women spend **$15B+ annually** on menopause products, often guessing what works

The experience is chaotic: hot flashes, night sweats, brain fog, insomnia, mood swings, joint pain, weight changes, irregular cycles, anxiety — hitting different women differently, in combinations that feel random.

**Women don't know:**
- What stage they're in (perimenopause lasts 4-8 years and has distinct phases)
- What triggers their symptoms (food, stress, alcohol, weather, sleep)
- Whether treatments are working (HRT takes 4-12 weeks to show effects)
- What's "normal" vs. what needs medical attention
- How to communicate symptoms effectively to dismissive doctors

## The Solution: Ember

Ember is the first AI-powered perimenopause & menopause companion that **finds patterns in your symptoms** so you can take control.

### How It Works

1. **Quick daily check-in** (90 seconds): Log hot flashes, sleep quality, mood, energy, and relevant factors (food, alcohol, stress, exercise, supplements)
2. **AI trigger detection** runs weekly: "Your hot flashes spike 2.7x on days after you drink wine. Your sleep improves 40% on days you exercise before noon."
3. **Stage prediction**: Machine learning models estimate your perimenopause stage (early, late, transition, post) based on cycle + symptom patterns
4. **Doctor-ready report**: One-tap export of a clinical summary with symptom frequency, severity trends, treatment history, and correlation data
5. **Treatment tracker**: Log HRT, supplements, lifestyle changes. Ember tracks whether they're working over 4-12 week windows
6. **Community insights**: "73% of women in late perimenopause with your symptom profile reported improvement with X" (anonymized, evidence-based)

### Design Philosophy

Menopause apps have historically been either:
- **Clinical and sterile** (like reading a textbook)
- **Patronizing and pink** (flowers, soft fonts, condescending tone)

Ember is neither. It's **warm, direct, and intelligent** — like talking to a sharp friend who's been through it. The design uses deep amber and charcoal (not pink), clean data viz (not vague infographics), and treats the user as an adult managing a complex transition.

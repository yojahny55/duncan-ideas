📓 Evidence timeline

⏳ 30-day dispute window — FDCPA §809(b) / 15 U.S.C. §1692g(b)

From the collector's first contact, you have 30 days to dispute the debt in writing. Dispute within the window and they must stop all collection until they mail you validation. Miss it, and the debt is presumed valid.

👩‍⚖️ Consumer attorney checklist

FDCPA cases are often taken on contingency (fees shift to the collector if you win). Bring your exported JSON + case summary.

  1. Find NACA-member attorneys: consumeradvocates.org → "Find an Attorney" (National Association of Consumer Advocates).
  2. Also search your state bar's consumer-protection section and Legal Aid if income-eligible.
  3. Before the call: CeaseFire → Export JSON + 🖨 Case summary. That's your intake packet.
  4. Bring: every collector letter, voicemails, call-log screenshots, certified-mail receipts.
  5. Ask: statute of limitations on the debt, whether it was ever validated, §1692k statutory damages, fee arrangement.

📮 State Attorney General

Most states accept consumer complaints online (search "[your state] attorney general file a complaint"). Several states — CA (Rosenthal Act), NY, MA, IL — have collector laws broader than the FDCPA. A CFPB complaint + state AG complaint costs nothing and creates a paper trail regulators actually read.

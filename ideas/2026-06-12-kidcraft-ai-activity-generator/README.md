# KidCraft — AI Kids' Activity Generator 🎨

**"What do I do with the kids?" — Solved in 3 seconds.**

## The Problem

Every parent faces the same daily panic: "What am I going to do with the kids today?" Rainy days, school holidays, sick days, playdates, birthday parties — the demand for fresh, age-appropriate activities is relentless.

- **Parents spend 30+ minutes daily** searching Pinterest, Google, and parenting blogs for activity ideas
- **Most activities found online** require specific materials you don't have or are wrong for your kids' ages
- **"I'm bored!"** is the #1 phrase kids say during school breaks (source: every parent ever)
- **43M US households** with kids under 12 need constant activity inspiration
- Parents of **multiple kids** struggle to find activities that work for different age groups simultaneously

**The gap:** Pinterest shows pretty pictures. Google gives blog posts with 47 ads. Parenting apps track schedules and milestones. Nobody generates **instant, personalized activity plans** based on what you actually have available.

## The Solution

**KidCraft** is an AI-powered activity generator for parents. Tell it your kids' ages, what materials you have, how much time you've got, and whether you're indoors or outdoors — get a complete activity plan with step-by-step instructions in 3 seconds.

### How It Works
1. **Quick Setup** — Enter kids' ages (one-time), select your situation (rainy day, playdate, birthday, etc.)
2. **Smart Input** — "I have a 4-year-old and 7-year-old, 45 minutes, indoor, household items only"
3. **AI Magic** — Get 3 personalized activities with instructions, materials, difficulty, and cleanup estimate
4. **Save & Track** — Heart favorites, mark completed, build a personal activity library

## Key Features

### 🎯 Instant Activity Generator
- AI creates custom activities based on your exact situation
- Adapts to multiple kids at different ages simultaneously
- Considers time available (5 min to full day)
- Indoor / outdoor / both options

### 📦 What You Have Filter
- Select from common household items (paper, markers, tape, cardboard, kitchen supplies, etc.)
- Never suggests activities requiring stuff you don't own
- "No prep" mode: activities requiring zero materials

### ⏱️ Smart Metadata
- **Time estimate** (prep + activity + cleanup)
- **Mess level** (🟢 Clean → 🔴 Cover the furniture)
- **Energy level** (Chill → Active → Exhaust them)
- **Age range** with specific adaptations per age
- **Solo / partnered / group** mode

### 💾 Activity Library
- Save favorites to your family's collection
- "We did this!" tracking with optional photos
- History log: what worked, what flopped
- Seasonal/holiday-themed suggestions

### 🎪 Situation Modes
- **Rainy Day** — indoor, high engagement
- **Playdate** — multi-kid collaborative activities
- **Birthday Party** — group games & crafts with themes
- **Road Trip** — car-friendly, no-mess activities
- **Bedtime Wind-Down** — calming, low-energy
- **School Break Survival** — week-long activity calendars
- **Sick Day** — low-energy, couch-friendly
- **10-Minute Fillers** — quick transitions between things

### 📊 Parent Intelligence
- "What worked" ratings after each activity
- AI learns your kids' preferences over time
- Age-appropriate skill development tracking (fine motor, creativity, STEM, social)
- Weekly activity digest

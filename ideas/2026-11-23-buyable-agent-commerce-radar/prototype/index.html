TRX-002HIGH · BLOCKS CHATGPT + ALLCAPTCHA before payment

Challenge detected on checkout step 1 (bot protection at the payment boundary). Most agents stop here permanently. Human shoppers barely notice; your agents never came back.

REMOVE challenge from guest checkout path, or scope bot protection to failed attempts only. Shopify: Apps → [bot-manager] → exclude /checkout/* · then re-walk.
EVAL-005MEDIUM · BLOCKS GEMINI BUY BUTTONReturns not machine-readable

Return policy lives only on a human policy page. Gemini's Buy button requires hasMerchantReturnPolicy on Product markup.

ADD to Product JSON-LD: hasMerchantReturnPolicy { returnPolicyCategory: ReturnFiniteReturnWindow, merchantReturnDays: 30, applicableCountry: US }
DISC-004LOW · TRUST RISKStale JSON-LD prices (14 products)

Sale price renders on page; markup still carries the pre-sale price. 14 SKUs, mostly the fall sale collection.

REGEN feed template — price field maps to sale_price when set. Affected SKUs: TR2-BLK-42, TR2-GRY-40, … (full list in hand-off).

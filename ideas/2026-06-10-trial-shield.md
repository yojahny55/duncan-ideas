# TrialShield — Never Pay for a Forgotten Free Trial Again

**Date:** June 10, 2026
**Source:** r/AppIdeas — 40+ threads about unwanted subscription charges
**Status:** Idea + Prototype

---

## Problem Statement

People sign up for free trials (7-day, 14-day, 30-day) and forget to cancel. They get charged $9.99–$49.99/month for services they never used after day one. The average person has **3–5 dormant subscriptions** costing $200–$600/year. Banks don't alert you until the charge hits. Calendar reminders get ignored. The subscription economy profits from forgetfulness.

**Pain points:**
- No central view of *when* trials expire across all services
- Credit card statements are opaque — charges blend in
- Cancelling is intentionally difficult (dark patterns)
- People feel stupid/frustrated when they realize they've been paying for months

## Solution

**TrialShield** — a smart trial & subscription tracker that:

1. **Detects trials automatically** by scanning email for signup confirmations (Gmail/Outlook OAuth)
2. **Countdown dashboard** — shows days remaining on every active trial with urgency colors (green → yellow → red)
3. **One-tap cancel reminders** — push notification 48h before trial ends, with direct link to cancel page
4. **Charge alerts** — notifies when a subscription charge hits your card (bank integration via Plaid)
5. **"Burn rate" calculator** — shows total monthly/yearly subscription spend
6. **Cancel assistance** — AI walks you through cancellation steps for each service, or generates cancel emails

### Killer Feature: Trial Card
A virtual prepaid card (via Stripe) that auto-declines after the trial period. Sign up for trials without *ever* risking a charge. The card literally stops working.

## Target Users

- **Primary:** 25–40 year old professionals with 5+ SaaS subscriptions
- **Secondary:** College students trying tools for projects (heavy trial users)
- **Tertiary:** Freelancers managing business expenses
- **Market size:** ~180M subscription-active users in the US alone

## Monetization

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Manual tracking, basic reminders |
| **Pro** | $4.99/mo | Auto-detection via email, charge alerts, cancel assist |
| **Premium** | $9.99/mo | Everything + Trial Card (virtual card), family sharing, analytics |
| **B2B** | Custom | Team subscription management for small companies |

**Unit economics:** Plaid + Stripe costs ~$0.50/user/month. Premium at $9.99 = strong margin.

**Other revenue:**
- Affiliate: partner with competing services (e.g., "Switch from Adobe to Canva, get 3 months free")
- Anonymous subscription trend data (aggregated, no PII)

## Competitive Edge

- **Trim/Rocket Money** focus on existing subscriptions, not *trial prevention*
- **TrialPay** doesn't exist anymore — gap in market
- No one offers a **virtual trial card** that auto-expires

☠ WHY THIS STEP MATTERS

If you skip the structure and hire wrong, the U.S. Dept. of Labor says it costs $11,220 — 30% of first-year earnings on this role — plus the months of owner time re-doing their work. The interview kit above costs you two hours. Do the math; the math is lopsided.

First 30 Days — Land the Plane

  • Day 1–3: Complete Form I-9 (within 3 business days of start) & collect W-4.
  • Week 1: Register with state new-hire reporting program (deadline varies, usually 20 days).
  • Week 1: Workers' comp policy active before first shift. Payroll running — Gusto/ADP referral handoff.
  • Week 2: Owner-shadowing: they watch you do each triaged HIRE task once.
  • Week 3–4: Reverse-shadow: you watch them. Score against the same rubric.
  • Day 30: Checkpoint against the scorecard. Celebrate or course-correct — in writing.

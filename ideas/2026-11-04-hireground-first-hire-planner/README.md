## 2. Problem Statement

The first hire is the hardest, least-supported hiring decision an owner ever makes:

1. **No framework for *what* to hire for.** Owners feel "overwhelmed" but can't decompose their week into tasks, hours, and skills. They hire a vague "assistant" and get a vague employee.
2. **They don't know the true cost.** Sticker wage ≠ cost. DOL puts a **bad hire at 30% of first-year earnings** (~$24k on an $80k role; ~$11k on a $37k part-timer). SHRM's 2025–26 benchmarks: median cost-per-hire **$1,300** (recruiting only); Center for American Progress: replacing an employee costs **~20% of salary**; Zippia (Jan 2026): **$3,500+** all-in to recruit and hire.
3. **W-2 vs 1099 vs fractional vs AI is a fog.** Misclassifying a worker carries IRS/state penalties; over-automating loses the point of hiring.
4. **The posting and interview are improvised.** Owners copy a Indeed template, wing the interview with "tell me about yourself," and select for charisma. Structured interviews are the single best-documented bad-hire mitigant — and no consumer tool delivers one to a 2-person shop.
5. **Compliance creeps in.** Pay-transparency laws now require salary ranges in postings in CA, NY, CO, WA, IL, NJ and a growing list of states/localities; new-hire paperwork (I-9, W-4, state new-hire reporting) has hard deadlines. Owners learn this the hard way.

**The result:** ~30M employee-less small businesses stay solo longer than they should (losing owner time = the scarcest asset), or hire badly and pay the 30% tax.

## 4. The Solution — HireGround

A guided planner (web PWA, ~45 minutes end-to-end) with seven beats:

1. **Unload** — Owner brain-dumps every recurring task with hours/week (typed or voice → text).
2. **Triage** — Each task gets classified **Automate / Outsource / Hire**, with a concrete recommendation ("posting to social → a $29/mo scheduler saves 5 hrs/wk; bookkeeping → a $150/mo service"). This is the 2026 differentiator: *you may need AI and a bookkeeper before you need an employee.*
3. **Scorecard** — The surviving "Hire" tasks auto-compose into a role: suggested title, hours/week, required skills checklist, seniority, full-time vs part-time verdict.
4. **Budget** — True fully-loaded cost: wage × hours + FICA (7.65%) + workers' comp + unemployment + benefits stipend + one-time equipment/onboarding; side-by-side **W-2 vs 1099 vs fractional**; cost-per-hour-freed; a plain-English "can you afford this yet?" verdict against revenue inputs.
5. **Post** — Job posting generator with **pay-transparency compliance by state** (range auto-inserted where required), realistic "employee #2" framing that actually attracts good candidates.
6. **Interview** — Structured kit: task-derived questions, behavioral bank, a paid work-sample exercise design, and a 1–5 scoring rubric so two candidates are comparable.
7. **Land** — First-30-days onboarding checklist + paperwork timeline (I-9 within 3 days, W-4, state new-hire reporting deadlines) + payroll-provider handoff.

**Positioning line:** *TurboTax for your first hire.*

## 5. What Exists Today (and Why It Fails)

| Incumbent | What it does | The gap |
|---|---|---|
| Indeed / ZipRecruiter | Post jobs, collect applicants | Starts *after* the decision; no role design, no cost model |
| Gusto / ADP / Rippling | Payroll & HR admin | Starts *after* the offer; enterprise-shaped UX |
| PEOs (Justworks, Gusto PEO) | Co-employment HR bundle | $100+/employee/mo + setup; built for teams of 5+, not hire #1 |
| SHRM / SCORE templates | Static PDFs & articles | Generic, no math, no state logic, no triage |
| ChatGPT | Anything, sort of | No fully-loaded cost model, no compliance dataset, no artifacts, no accountability |
| HR consultants | Bespoke advice | $150–300/hr — more than the first employee costs |

**Nobody owns the pre-decision moment.** That's HireGround's wedge, and it's a *natural* lead-gen position for everything downstream (payroll, benefits, insurance).

## 7. Monetization

1. **Free (lead magnet):** task triage + budget calculator, email-gated results. This is also the cheap validation test PainPoint Daily prescribes: *pass if a meaningful share complete a full scenario.*
2. **$79 one-time — First Hire Kit:** scorecard + posting + interview kit + onboarding checklists, exportable (PDF/Docs). One-time pricing matches the one-time event.
3. **$29/mo — Growing Team:** hire #2, #3… role library, HR doc templates, offer-letter generator, annual comp updates. Converts the successful first experience.
4. **Affiliate/referral (likely the biggest early revenue):** payroll (Gusto/ADP referral programs historically $100–500/referral), job boards, workers' comp insurance, bookkeeping services recommended *in* the triage step — the recommendation engine is natively monetizable.
5. **B2B licensing:** white-label the planner for SCORE chapters, chambers, franchisors, and bank SMB teams.

Unit economics: LLM cost per completed plan ≈ $0.10–0.40. At $79 with even 3% free→paid conversion plus one payroll referral per 10 kits, CAC via Reddit/SEO content ("how much does my first employee really cost calculator") stays near zero.

## 8. Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** Next.js 15 + TypeScript + Tailwind; PWA (owners live on phones); the prototype below is the design north star.
- **AI:** Claude API with structured outputs — task triage classification, title suggestion, posting prose, question generation. Heuristic fallback (keyword rules) for the free tier so it works with zero LLM cost.
- **Data:** static dataset of state/local pay-transparency rules + new-hire paperwork deadlines (NCSL/DOL-sourced, refreshed quarterly); cost-model constants (FICA 7.65%, FUTA/SUTA, workers' comp class-code ranges).
- **Backend:** Supabase (auth, plans, Stripe webhooks); Stripe one-time + subscriptions; email via Resend (kit delivery).
- **Deploy:** Vercel + Supabase; Plausible for funnel analytics (free-tool completion rate is the KPI).

## 10. Design & Prototype

**Aesthetic direction:** *"1940s employment office, letterpress edition"* — cream paper stock, typewriter forms, ink-black rules, and a red rubber stamp that thunks down when your budget checks out. Hiring person #1 should feel like official business, not another SaaS dashboard. Display: Alfa Slab One ("HELP WANTED" energy). Forms: Courier Prime. Body: Karla.

`prototype/index.html` — single-file, no build step, fully working 5-step wizard:

1. **The Overload** — task dump with hours/week (pre-seeded realistic sample).
2. **The Triage** — click-to-cycle Automate/Outsource/Hire badges with live bucket totals.
3. **The Budget** — wage slider → fully-loaded monthly/annual cost, W-2 vs 1099 comparison, cost-per-hour-freed, and the verdict **stamp** (FULL-TIME READY / PART-TIME SWEET SPOT / NOT YET).
4. **The Posting** — generated job post with pay-transparency flag by state, copy-to-clipboard.
5. **The Interview** — task-derived structured questions, work-sample design, 1–5 scoring rubric, and the bad-hire-cost callout (30% of first-year earnings, DOL).

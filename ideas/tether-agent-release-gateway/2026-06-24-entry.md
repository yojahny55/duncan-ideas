## Insight

Observability tools (LangSmith, Helicone, Braintrust, FutureAGI) tell you what happened *after* your agent burns customers. Nobody owns the moment *before* deployment — the equivalent of CI/CD for code, but for agent behavior.

## The idea

**Tether** = "GitHub Actions for AI agents."
- Record a golden suite of agent scenarios once
- On every change (prompt, model, tool schema, framework bump), replay in sandbox
- Get a side-by-side behavior diff: tool calls, hallucinations, latency, cost
- Verdict (✅ / ⚠️ / ❌) gates the release in your existing CI

## Why it's differentiated

| Tool | Pre-deploy? | Behavior diff? | Release gate? |
|---|---|---|---|
| LangSmith | manual | partial | ❌ |
| Helicone | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Braintrust | manual | partial | ❌ |

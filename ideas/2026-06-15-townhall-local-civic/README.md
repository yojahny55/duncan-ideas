# TownHall 🏛️ — Local Politics for Busy People

> **"Your city council is voting on things that affect you this week. You just don't know it."**

---

## 💡 The Problem

Local government makes the decisions that most directly shape your life — property taxes, school boundaries, zoning, parking permits, rental laws, public transit, police budgets, restaurant hours, noise rules, tree removal, short-term rentals, and on and on.

But almost nobody knows what their city council, school board, or county commissioners are voting on until **after** the vote happens.

**Why? Because local government information is:**

1. **Buried** — Agendas live as 80-page PDFs on a city website from 2009
2. **Unsummarized** — No plain-English version exists
3. **Fragmented** — City council here, school board there, county over there
4. **Unaware** — No notification when something you care about is on the agenda
5. **Inaccessible** — Meetings held at 10 AM on Tuesdays in a basement chamber

**Result:** A tiny slice of engaged residents (typically older, retired, homeowners) dominate local decisions. The other 95% of residents — including everyone under 45, renters, working parents, small business owners — are functionally excluded from the decisions that shape their daily lives.

---

## 🎯 Target Users

**Primary:** Busy professionals and parents aged 25–55 who care about their community but can't attend 10 AM Tuesday council meetings.

**Segments:**
- **Young homeowners** — property tax assessments, zoning changes, school boundaries
- **Renters** — rent stabilization, eviction rules, short-term rental permits, noise ordinances
- **Parents** — school board decisions, bond measures, curriculum, attendance zones
- **Small business owners** — licensing, signage rules, outdoor dining permits, minimum wage
- **Civic-curious** — people who want to be involved but don't know where to start
- **Local journalists** — need a feed of what's happening across beats
- **Advocacy groups** — housing, transit, environment, civil rights

**Demographics:** 89M+ registered voters in the US. 19,000+ incorporated municipalities. 500K+ elected officials. Nearly everyone is affected by local government; almost no one is informed about it.

---

## ✨ The Solution

**TownHall** is a personalized local civic feed. Enter your address. Get a clear, plain-English feed of what your local government is voting on this week, who your reps are, how they've voted, and one-tap ways to make your voice heard — in under 60 seconds.

### Core Features

**1. 📍 Rep Finder**
- Enter your address → see every rep who represents you
- City council, school board, county commission, state legislature, Congress
- Photos, bios, term lengths, campaign contact links, social handles
- "Last voted with you?" alignment score based on your stated values

**2. 📅 This Week in Your Town**
- Upcoming meetings + votes with one-sentence summaries
- "Why this matters" impact analysis (property taxes, schools, commute, rent)
- Filter by topic: zoning, schools, taxes, public safety, transit, housing, environment
- Calendar export + smart notifications ("Agenda item 7 affects your block")

**3. 🗣️ Make Your Voice Heard**
- One-tap email your rep with AI-personalized, professional message
- Call script generator (reads the bill, generates 30-second talking points)
- Public comment submitter where jurisdictions allow it
- Tweet/Facebook post generator for sharing with neighbors
- Sample scripts for first-time speakers at meetings

**4. 📊 Vote Tracker**
- How your reps actually voted on key issues
- Attendance record (missed votes matter)
- "Flip-flop" detector — when did they change positions?
- Donor tracking (who funds them? OpenSecrets integration)
- Compare reps head-to-head

**5. 🗳️ Election Center**
- Local election dates and deadlines
- Sample ballots as they become available
- Candidate Q&A (AI-formatted from public forums + candidate surveys)
- Voter registration status check
- Absentee/mail-in ballot request
- Polling location + hours

**6. 📈 Civic Score (Streaks)**
- Track your civic engagement actions
- Build streaks: emails sent, calls made, comments submitted
- Compare your district's engagement to others
- "Civic health" score for your ZIP code

**7. 🚨 Topic Alerts**
- Subscribe to topics: "renter protections," "school boundaries," "tree removal," "bike lanes," "short-term rentals"
- Get pinged the moment a matching agenda item appears anywhere in your jurisdiction
- Custom keyword alerts (e.g., "your street name")

## 📊 Market Research

### Why Now (June 2026)

- **Local news collapse** — 1/3 of US newspapers have shuttered since 2005. 70M+ Americans live in "news deserts" with no daily local journalism. TownHall fills the information vacuum.
- **Federal gridlock** — With DC stalled, the consequential action has shifted to cities and states. Rent control, zoning reform, climate policy — all being fought locally.
- **AI summaries are finally good enough** — 80-page council agenda PDFs can be crisply summarized by Claude/GPT-5/GLM-5 in 2026. This was impossible in 2022.
- **Post-Roe / post-Dobbs civic awareness** — People realized state and local elections have massive stakes.
- **Nextdoor fatigue** — Users are exhausted by neighborhood gossip and want substantive civic info.
- **Gen Z civic engagement** — Highest youth turnout in generations; they expect digital-first civic tools.

### Existing Players (and Why They Leave the Gap Open)

| Tool | What It Does | Why It's Not Enough |
|---|---|---|
| **Nextdoor** | Neighborhood social network | Gossip-focused, no agenda/vote tracking |
| **SeeClickFix** | 311 pothole/graffiti reports | Service requests only, not policy |
| **Vote.org** | Voter registration | One-time use, not ongoing civic feed |
| **Ballotpedia** | Encyclopedia of elections | Reference, not feed; overwhelming |
| **5 Calls** | National Congress calls | Federal only, ignores local |
| **OpenStates** | State legislation data | State-level only, developer-facing |
| **Plural** | Legislative tracking | B2B, expensive, sold to lobbyists |
| **PowerDMS / iCompass** | Government management | Sold to clerks, not residents |
| **CivicEagle** | Bill tracking | State/federal, no local depth |
| **Countable** | Federal legislation advocacy | Shut down 2024 — was never local |

**The gap:** Nobody has built a consumer-grade, personalized feed of *local* government actions. The space is a graveyard of abandoned attempts and B2B-only tools.

### Market Size

- **89M** registered US voters
- **19,500** incorporated municipalities
- **3,000+** counties
- **13,500** independent school districts
- **500K+** elected officials
- **1M+** public meetings per year in the US
- **$7.5B** global govtech market (mostly B2B — consumer side is wide open)
- **73%** of Americans can't name their city council representative (Pew 2024)
- **62%** of Americans say they don't have enough information to participate locally

## 🛠️ Technical Approach

### Data Sourcing

**Tier 1 — Aggregators (fast launch):**
- OpenStates API (state legislation)
- Google Civic Information API (rep lookup)
- Vote Smart API (candidate positions)
- Ballotpedia API (where available)

**Tier 2 — Direct Scraping (per city):**
- LLM-assisted scraper: feed city clerk websites → Claude extracts structured agenda items
- Legistar integration (used by ~2,500 of the largest US cities)
- PDF → Markdown → LLM summarization pipeline

**Tier 3 — Partnerships (long-term moat):**
- Partner with state municipal leagues for structured data
- Partner with local newsrooms for shared coverage
- Public records requests for historical vote data

### Tech Stack (Solo Dev Buildable)

- **Mobile-first web app** → React Native or Expo (cross-platform)
- **Backend:** Supabase (Postgres + Auth + Storage)
- **AI:** Claude 4 / GPT-5 / GLM-5 for summarization and message generation
- **Search:** Postgres full-text + pgvector for semantic agenda search
- **Data pipeline:** Python + Playwright + LiteLLM
- **Hosting:** Vercel (app) + Railway (workers) + Supabase (DB)
- **Notifications:** OneSignal (push), Postmark (email)

### MVP Scope (8-Week Build)

**Week 1–2:** Rep finder, address lookup, city council agenda scraper (Tampa as launch city)
**Week 3–4:** Weekly feed, topic alerts, basic rep profiles
**Week 5–6:** Email/call scripts, vote tracker, election center
**Week 7–8:** Polish, onboarding, App Store submission

**Launch city:** Tampa, FL (Yojahny's backyard — dogfood locally first).

## 🎨 Design Principles

1. **Nonpartisan by default** — Show facts, votes, and summaries; never endorsements
2. **60-second value** — From signup to "I know what's happening this week" in under a minute
3. **Local-first** — National politics is noise; your school board is signal
4. **Plain English** — No "WHEREAS" clauses, no legalese, no jargon
5. **Action-oriented** — Every agenda item ends with "Here's what you can do"
6. **Respect attention** — Weekly digest by default, not firehose notifications

## 🤝 The Bigger Vision

Local democracy in America is quietly dying — not from malice, but from inattention. The people who show up to city council meetings are not representative of the community. The decisions still get made, but without most residents' input.

TownHall's mission: **make local civic participation take 5 minutes a week, not 5 hours.**

If we succeed, we rewire how local democracy works. More voices heard, more accountability, better decisions, stronger communities.

That's worth building.
